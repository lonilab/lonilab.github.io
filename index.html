About LoNI

The Laboratory of Neurodevelopmental Informatics (LoNI) is dedicated to understanding how early-life exposures shape brain maturation and developmental trajectories through computational and data-driven approaches, with the goal of improving early detection of developmental risk, informing preventive strategies, and supporting targeted interventions that promote healthier developmental outcomes.
