Welcome to LoNI

The Laboratory of Neurodevelopmental Informatics studies how early-life exposures shape brain maturation and developmental trajectories through computational and data-driven approaches.

Our work integrates developmental science, neuroscience, and informatics to improve early detection of developmental risk, inform preventive strategies, and support targeted interventions that promote healthier developmental outcomes.

Research Focus

We investigate neurodevelopment across childhood and adolescence using neuroimaging, longitudinal cohorts, and computational modelling.

Developmental neuroscience Neuroinformatics Mental health

Featured Direction

Current work examines how adversity, stress, and environmental exposures shape brain development and mental health risk, with a focus on early identification and precision intervention.
