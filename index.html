Welcome to Our Lab

Our primary research lies in developmental science. We examine how early-life adversity, and other environmental exposures shape brain maturation and developmental trajectories. We also investigate how emerging technologies can improve the assessment, prediction, and intervention of mental health. This includes the use of mobile health applications to monitor behavior and subjective experiences in real time, and the development of predictive models to identify clinical risks associated with developmental deviation, including psychosis risk, treatment resistance, and other adverse outcomes. Our goal is to support early identification, risk stratification, and precision intervention. Our research adopts an interdisciplinary approach that integrates psychiatry, psychology, and neuroscience to advance understanding of developmental processes and the mechanisms underlying mental health risk.
