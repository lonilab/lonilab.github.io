Welcome to Our Lab

The Laboratory of Neurodevelopmetnal Informatics (LoNI) is dedicated to understanding how early-life exposures shape brain maturation and developmental trajectories through computational and data-driven approaches. Our work aims to inform early detection of developmental risk, guide preventive strategies, and support targeted interventions that promote healthier developmental outcomes.
