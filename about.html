About the Lab

The Laboratory of Neurodevelopmental Informatics (LoNI) focuses on how early-life exposures shape brain maturation and developmental trajectories through computational and data-driven approaches, with the goal of improving early detection of developmental risk, informing preventive strategies, and supporting targeted interventions that promote healthier developmental outcomes.

Our Aim

We aim to advance understanding of neurodevelopment by integrating developmental science, psychiatry, psychology, neuroscience, and informatics. Our work uses neuroimaging, longitudinal data, and computational methods to identify developmental risk and resilience mechanisms across childhood and adolescence.

What We Do

Developmental Neuroscience

We study how environmental and psychosocial exposures influence brain maturation and developmental trajectories.

Neuroinformatics

We develop and apply computational, statistical, and data-driven methods to analyse complex developmental and neuroimaging data.

Mental Health Risk

We investigate mechanisms underlying developmental and mental health risk, with emphasis on early identification and precision intervention.
