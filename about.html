About the Lab

Our Aim

We aim to advance understanding of human development and promote healthier developmental outcomes by integrating developmental science, neuroscience, and informatics. Through neuroimaging, longitudinal data, and computational methods, we seek to uncover the mechanisms of developmental risk and resilience across childhood and adolescence and translate this knowledge into earlier identification, prevention, and more targeted intervention.

What We Do

Developmental Neuroscience

We study how environmental and psychosocial exposures influence brain maturation and developmental trajectories.

Neuroinformatics

We develop and apply computational, statistical, and data-driven methods to analyse complex developmental and neuroimaging data.

Mental Health Risk

We investigate mechanisms underlying developmental and mental health risk, with emphasis on early identification and precision intervention.
