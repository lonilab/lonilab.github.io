Our project, “Investigating Resilient Psychosocial Functioning in Youth Using 
Functional Brain Connectivity and Digital Phenotyping: Are Individuals with 
Higher Resilience More Distinctive?”, has been awarded funding under the RGC 
ECS scheme. he project investigates whether higher psychosocial resilience in 
youth is linked to greater individual distinctiveness in functional brain 
connectivity and digital phenotyping. By combining fMRI and real-world 
behavioral data, it seeks to identify person-specific signatures of resilience 
and to clarify how resilient functioning can be characterized at the individual 
level.
